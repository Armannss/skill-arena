Automatic follow-ups

Stop writing “just checking in” emails.

Chasing money is the worst part of freelancing. It eats your time and makes things weird with good clients. Let Ledgerly do the nagging so you don’t have to.

  • Your schedule

    Choose when reminders go out: on the due date, a few days late, two weeks late.

  • Your words

    Edit every reminder so it sounds like you, from friendly to firm.

  • Stops when they pay

    The moment an invoice is paid, the follow-ups end. Nobody gets nudged by mistake.

  • Pause any client

    Agreed on a payment plan? Turn reminders off for that invoice with one click.

Questions

Good to know.

Is the free plan actually free?

Yes. Up to 3 clients, no time limit and no credit card. You only pay if you upgrade to Pro.

What happens when I add a fourth client?

Ledgerly will ask you to upgrade to Pro at $12 a month. Your existing clients, invoices and reminders stay exactly as they are.

Will automatic reminders annoy my clients?

You decide when reminders go out and what they say. They stop the moment an invoice is paid, and you can pause them for any invoice. Most clients simply forgot, and a polite nudge is all it takes.

Do my clients need a Ledgerly account?

No. They get an email with a link to view the invoice and pay. Nothing to sign up for.

Can I cancel Pro?

Anytime, from your account settings. No contracts and no cancellation fees.
