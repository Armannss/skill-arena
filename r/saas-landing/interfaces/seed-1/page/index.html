FAQ

Questions, answered

Is the free plan really free?

Yes. No credit card, no trial clock. You can invoice up to 3 clients for as long as you like.

What counts as a client?

A client is any person or business you send invoices to. You can send as many invoices as you need to each one.

Will the reminders sound pushy?

Only if you want them to. You choose when each reminder goes out and can edit every message before turning it on. The default wording is polite and to the point.

What happens when I add a fourth client?

Ledgerly will ask you to move to Pro for $12 a month. Invoices and reminders for your existing clients keep working either way.

Can I cancel Pro at any time?

Yes. Cancel from your settings and you'll drop back to the free plan at the end of your billing month.
