Questions about plans

What does the free plan include?
Sending invoices, automatic reminders and the overview of what you are owed, for up to 3 clients.
When do I need Pro?
When you invoice a fourth client. Pro is $12 a month.
When do reminders stop?
As soon as you mark an invoice as paid. Until then they go out on the days you set.
Can I change the reminder days?
Yes. Pick any days after the due date, for example 1, 7 and 14.
